Report format
The four Back Office formats (Overall Rollup, Detailed Survey Report, Surveys Requiring Action, TE Detail Comments), plus the Consultant Scorecard for WOW / ACE and the Survey Status list asked for by Lorrie.
Corporate client, Service provider, Division, Policy, Transferee, Move type
Keep only surveys that match. Service provider is the consultant on the file (Back Office: Primary Contact).
Date range
Uses Survey Sent Date. Pick a quarter or a year in Quick range so the scorecard can show the WOW / ACE award for that period.
Show metric box
Same as the Back Office check boxes: which of Top Box, Top 2 Box and Top 3 Box to show.

Source

Rating scale
Very Dissatisfied (1 star) to Very Satisfied (5 stars). Boxes are counted from the rating label, so SE storing the points the other way round does not change them.
Top Box
Very Satisfied ÷ all ratings.
Top 2 Box
Very Satisfied + Satisfied ÷ all ratings.
Top 3 Box
Very Satisfied + Satisfied + Somewhat Satisfied ÷ all ratings.
Return rate
As in Back Office: TEs who answered a survey sent in the range ÷ TEs sent a survey in the range.
Returned
The survey has at least one answer.
Negative response
Dissatisfied, Very Dissatisfied, or No on a yes/no question. To confirm with Bert.
Categories
Overall: the NuCompass section and any "recommend" question. Knowledgeable: questions about knowledge. Responsiveness: questions about responsiveness. Expense Processing: the Expense Management section.
Remove From Calc
Shown on the detailed report, left out of every count and box.
