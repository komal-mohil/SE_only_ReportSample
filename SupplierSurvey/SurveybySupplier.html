Field and value notes
Source
WarehouseDB.dbo.FactTransfereeSurvey, a copy of SE's udv_transferee_survey_view_34: the same data as the Transferee Survey report.
Supplier question
Any question outside the NuCompass sections (NuCompass Mobility Inc., NuCompass Consultant, Expense Management, CoPilot, Other Comments). In SE these are the service sections: Household Goods, Auto Shipment, Temporary Living, Training, Destination Services.
Rating scale
Very Dissatisfied (1 star) to Very Satisfied (5 stars). Back Office used Strongly Disagree to Strongly Agree.
Top Box
Very Satisfied ÷ all answers. Top 2 adds Satisfied, Top 3 adds Somewhat Satisfied. Counted from the label, not the points.
Yes / No questions (**)
Yes counts under Satisfied and No under Dissatisfied, as Back Office put Yes under Agree and No under Disagree.
(VIP)
Client VIP or NCM VIP is Yes on the file.
Remove From Report / Calc
Remove From Report hides the answer; Remove From Calc shows it but leaves it out of the counts.
